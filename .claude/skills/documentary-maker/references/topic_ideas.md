# Topic Ideas Bank — Real Western Stories (Berkshires-Style)
Present these to the user in PHASE 0. Every story is real & verifiable, with strong
archival photo availability (Wikimedia Commons / Library of Congress). Each follows the
formula: Rise → Peak → Fall → Ironic Twist (Chekhov's Gun) → Legacy.
Extend/refresh this list with web_search when needed.

## 🇺🇸 AMERICA
1. **Centralia, Pennsylvania — The Town That Has Been Burning for 60 Years**
   1962 landfill fire entered a coal seam; still burning, will burn ~250 more years.
   1,000+ residents → 5 today. Government deleted its ZIP code.
   *Chekhov's Gun:* the ignored 1962 fire-warning report.
2. **The Borscht Belt — Where America's Vacation Empire Vanished**
   500+ Catskills luxury resorts (Grossinger's served 150,000 meals a night).
   Cheap air travel killed the entire empire within 20 years; ruins remain.
3. **Kirkbride Asylums — The Palaces Built for the Insane**
   1800s America built castle-like asylums on the belief that "beauty heals."
   Overcrowding → horror → abandonment. Victorian giants now stand in ruin.
4. **North Brother Island — New York's Forgotten Island**
   10 minutes from Manhattan: Typhoid Mary's 26-year quarantine, the General Slocum
   disaster (1,000+ dead), and 60 years of total emptiness in the middle of NYC.

## 🇬🇧 UNITED KINGDOM
5. **Witley Court — England's Richest House, Ended in One Night**
   A Victorian palace that rivaled royalty. 1937 fire; the owner reportedly let it burn —
   insurance was cheaper. Now Europe's most haunting ruin.
6. **Imber & Tyneham — The Villages the Army "Borrowed" and Never Returned**
   1943: two entire villages evacuated for WWII training, promised return "after the war."
   80+ years later the Army still holds them; villagers died waiting.
   *Chekhov's Gun:* the note left on the church door — "Please treat the church kindly…"
7. **HMHS Britannic — The Titanic's Sister, Built Unsinkable, Sank Faster**
   Fitted with every post-Titanic fix (double hull, extra lifeboats). Became a WWI
   hospital ship and sank in 55 minutes — three times faster than Titanic.

## 🇮🇹 ITALY
8. **Craco — The Medieval City the Earth Swallowed**
   8,000-year-old hilltop city; a 1963 landslide (triggered by human error — sewer works)
   consumed it. Now a ghost town and Hollywood film set (Bond, Passion of the Christ).
9. **Consonno — Italy's "Las Vegas" That Never Opened**
   1960s: a millionaire bought and bulldozed a 1,000-year-old village to build a
   "City of Toys" — minarets, castles, dance halls. A landslide cut the road; the dream died.

## 🇩🇪 GERMANY
10. **Beelitz-Heilstätten — Hitler's Hospital, Then Soviet, Then Silence**
    60-building TB sanatorium empire; a wounded soldier named Adolf Hitler was treated
    there in 1916. Then 50 years as a Soviet military hospital. Empty since 1994 —
    surgery theatres still frozen in place.
11. **Wünsdorf — The "Forbidden City" Hidden Near Berlin**
    75,000 Soviets lived in a walled city inside East Germany — Germans banned from entry;
    a daily train ran to Moscow. In 1994 everyone left; Lenin's statue still stands.

## 🇫🇷 FRANCE
12. **Oradour-sur-Glane — The Village France Refused to Repair**
    1944: SS massacre, 642 dead, village burned. De Gaulle ordered it preserved untouched
    forever. Cars and sewing machines still sit frozen after 80 years.
    (⚠️ Sensitive — requires respectful, restrained treatment.)
13. **Château de la Mothe-Chandeniers — The Castle the Internet Bought**
    A fairy-tale castle abandoned to the forest after a 1932 fire — until 2017, when
    27,000 strangers crowdfunded its purchase at ~€50 each.
